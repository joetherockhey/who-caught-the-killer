Who caught the killer?

The Mentalist — Season 1, Episode 1

Patrick Jane is a consultant with no badge and no gun. The CBI detectives do the records, the forensics and the arrests. So who actually catches the killer? I scored all 23 episodes. This is the first.

The verdict — episode 1

80%Patrick Jane
20%The detectives

How the split is scored

Three weighted components, applied the same way to every episode: identification (50%) — who worked out who did it; evidence and legwork (30%) — forensics, records, canvassing, surveillance; resolution (20%) — who produced the confession or the arrest.

The detectivesReviewed the Red John files and proved the signature was faked. Lifted blood and a hair off the taunting note, which exposed an affair and produced a motive — pointing squarely at the wrong man.
Patrick JaneAsked a grieving father point blank if he killed his daughter. Lifted a therapist's office key during a hug. Planted a rumour about a diary that did not exist, then wrote it himself that night.

Why 80/20

The forensic evidence was real, and it was aimed at an innocent man. Jane identified the killer, manufactured the evidence and sprang the trap. The CBI contribution was the file review and the arrest — necessary, but not what broke the case.

The season so far

After episode 1: Jane 80%. Twenty-two to go. The running average moves every week — and in exactly one episode of the season, the police win outright.
